About Us
The Ministry of Civil Aviation (MoCA), Government of India, is responsible for formulating
national policies and programmes for the development and regulation of civil aviation in India.
It develops the policy and legislative framework for the orderly growth of air transport,
airport infrastructure and related aviation services.
