About Ministry
Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) came into existence as an independent Ministry on 15th October 1999 after the merger of the Department of Statistics and the Department of Programme Implementation. The Ministry is the nodal agency for planned and organized development of the statistical system in the country and coordination of statistical activities among different stakeholders in Government of India, State Governments, Union Territories as well as meeting requirements of the International Agencies.The Ministry has two wings viz. the Statistics Wing and the Programme Implementation (PI) Wing.

National Sample Survey
The National Sample Survey (NSS) conducts large-scale sample surveys on a wide range of socio-economic subjects across India
National Statistical Training Academy
NSSTA is an institute that conducts quality training programmes in the field of Official Statistics and other related subjects, using state of the art training techniques.
Indian Statistical Service
A cadre of Group A officers with core discipline of Statistics to control, coordinate, monitor and steer the diversified statistical system.
Subordinate Statistical Service
A cadre of Group B officers with core discipline of Statistics to assist in building crucial statistical database covering various aspects of Indian Economy.
National Statistical Commission
National Commission on Statistics to serve as a nodal and empowered body for all core statistical activities of the country
Data Governance
Data Governance creates frameworks that ensure for quality standards to meet international benchmarks developed through inter-ministerial statistical liaison efforts.
Sustainable Development Goals (SDG)
The SDGs are a comprehensive list of global goals integrating social, economic and environmental dimensions of development.
Administration
Administration Division is responsible for matters relating to General Administration, Official Language and such other matters pertaining to MoSPI.
Data Informatics & Innovation Division (DIID)
DIID is an attached office of the MoSPI supports with formulating and overarching framework for digital transformation of the MoSPI, including the plan for modernization of IT architecture.
Conference of Central and State Statistical Organizations
Provides a platform for discussion on statistical issues of common interest to the Central and State Statistical Organizations.
Resources for State DES
Refers to the tools, guidelines, and support materials provided by MOSPI to assist State Directorates of Economics and Statistics in data collection, compilation, and reporting activities.
