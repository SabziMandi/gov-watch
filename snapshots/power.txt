About Us
The Ministry of Power has been functioning as an independent ministry since 2 July 1992. Prior to this, it operated as the Ministry of Energy. Electricity is a Concurrent subject under Entry 38 of List III (Seventh Schedule) of the Constitution of India, enabling both the Union and State Governments to legislate on matters relating to electricity. The Ministry of Power is primarily responsible for the development of the electricity sector in the country.
