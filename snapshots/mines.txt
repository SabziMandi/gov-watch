About the Ministry
Shri G. Kishan Reddy
Hon'ble Union Minister of Coal and Mines, Govt. of India
Shri Satish Chandra Dubey
Hon'ble Minister of State for Coal and Mines, Govt. of India
The Ministry of Mines is responsible for the survey and exploration of all minerals (excluding natural gas, petroleum, and atomic minerals) and for the mining and metallurgy of non-ferrous metals such as aluminium, copper, zinc, lead, gold, and nickel; it is also responsible for the implementation and administration of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957, in respect of all mines and minerals other than coal, natural gas, and petroleum.
