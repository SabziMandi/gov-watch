About Us
Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs
The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), formerly known as the Central Board of Excise and
Customs (CBEC), is an apex body under the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance, Government of India,
headquartered at New Delhi.
CBIC is responsible for policy formulation relating to the levy and collection of Customs Duty, Central
Excise Duty, Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) and Integrated Goods and Services Tax (IGST),
prevention
of smuggling, and administration of matters concerning indirect taxes and narcotics within its
jurisdiction.
The Board also exercises administrative control over its field formations and subordinate offices,
including
Customs Houses, CGST and Central Excise Commissionerates, and the Central Revenues Control Laboratory.
Smt. Nirmala Sitharaman
