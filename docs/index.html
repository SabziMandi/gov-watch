+ Union Minister of Coal and Mines, Shri G. Kishan Reddy, in his address at the 4th Conference of Mining Ministers in Goa, emphasised strengthening India’s mineral security and self-reliance through Centre-State collaboration. Highlighting the sector’s progress, he noted that domestic coal production has crossed 1 billion tonnes and the production value of major minerals reached ₹1.89 lakh crore in FY 2025–26. He urged States to expedite the operationalisation of auctioned mines, accelerate exploration and strengthen the critical minerals ecosystem, including processing, refining and recycling. He also emphasised scientific mine closure, rehabilitation of mined-out areas and environmental sustainability, alongside rationalisation of mining taxes and adoption of advanced technologies.
