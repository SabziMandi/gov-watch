About MHI
The Ministry of Heavy Industry is concerned with the development of the Heavy Engineering and Machine Tools Industry, Heavy Electrical Engineering Industry, and Automotive Industry. It administers the following 40 Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) and their subsidiaries, as well as four autonomous bodies.
